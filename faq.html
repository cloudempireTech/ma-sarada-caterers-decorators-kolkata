FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Good questions make good plans.

These prompts help you prepare an enquiry. Specific answers should be confirmed with the business.

Can catering and decoration be discussed together?

The business is listed for both. Ask how the two services can be planned for your occasion and what is currently available.

Is there a fixed menu or package price?

No current menu or pricing could be verified for this concept. Request an up-to-date proposal directly from the business.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Share your event type, date, venue, approximate guest count and any food or décor ideas. Keep personal information to what is needed for a reply.

Are the photos current?

The gallery photos document earlier event displays. They should not be treated as proof of current dishes, personnel, availability or setup options.

How can I find the business?
